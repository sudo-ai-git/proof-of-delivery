VERDICT: the 4-layer commitment is NOT a security primitive. Three of its four layers are invariant under permutation, so its strength collapses to 32-bit FNV-1a — a full, concrete collision was found by brute force in seconds. It is retained only as a reproducible tamper-detection demo; file identity uses the sha256 kind.
